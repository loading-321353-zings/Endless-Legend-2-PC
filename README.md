## 🕹️ About

ENDLESS Legend™ 2 is a turn-based 4X fantasy strategy game developed by AMPLITUDE Studios and published by Hooded Horse. Set on Saiadha, an ocean world facing extinction, you lead an asymmetric faction from a humble settlement to a powerful empire. Explore a changing planet, expand cities, manage resources, research technologies, shape diplomacy, and command armies in tactical battles. Tidefalls, quests, heroes, and faction-specific abilities ensure that every campaign develops its own strategic story.

A strong fit for players who enjoy deep turn-based strategy, asymmetric factions, emergent storytelling, exploration, and long-form empire management.

## ✨ Features

- 🗺️ **4X Exploration** — Explore unknown regions, discover anomalies, investigate ruins, and uncover ancient secrets.
- 🦸 **Powerful Heroes** — Empower heroes who lead armies, shape faction stories, and help reveal the planet’s mysteries.
- 🤝 **Diplomacy and Influence** — Work with minor factions, build alliances, pursue political goals, or dominate rivals.
- 🏛️ **Asymmetric Factions** — Distinct philosophies, units, economies, narratives, and abilities create radically different playstyles.
- 🏙️ **Empire Building** — Develop cities with specialized districts, adjacency bonuses, defenses, and production networks.
- 📜 **Quests and Events** — Make consequential choices through faction narratives, quests, and events that alter campaigns.
- 🌊 **Living Ocean World** — Tidefalls reshape the map, revealing new territories, resources, routes, and dangers.
- ⚔️ **Tactical Warfare** — Raise armies and command troops in turn-based battles shaped by terrain, units, and faction strengths.
